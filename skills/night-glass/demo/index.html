FAQ

Do I need internet?

No. Orbi talks to your hub over Zigbee or Thread, right inside your home. Internet is only needed for updates.

Which devices are supported?

Anything your hub can see: lamps, relays, speakers, thermostats, blinds. If a device has a slider in its app, you can turn it with the knob.

How long does the battery last?

About six months with typical use. When 10% is left, the ring blinks once in the morning.

Can I use more than one?

Yes, one in every room if you like. Each remembers its own devices and its own color.

What if I don't have a hub?

Orbi works directly with up to eight Zigbee lamps. The other modes unlock when you add a hub.
