Journal/Guides/How to set up scenes

Guide

Set up scenes in five minutes

A scene remembers light, music and climate. Here is how to build your first one without getting lost in the rooms.

LWLeo White·Oct 19, 2026·6 min read

Contents

  1. What a scene is
  2. Your first scene
  3. Schedule

What a scene is

A scene is a snapshot of your home: which lamps are on, what is playing and what the temperature is. You can start it with a touch of the dial or on a schedule. Details are in the help center.

  • light: the brightness and color of each lamp;
  • music: the speaker and its volume;
  • climate: the target temperature.
Tip. Start with a single room: it is easier to see what got remembered.

Your first scene

  1. Set the light and music by hand.
  2. Hold the dial for two seconds.
  3. Choose Save as scene and give it a name.
The best scenes are born in the evening: set the house up your way and simply save what you end up with.

Schedule

Scenes can start at a set time. For example, this is how a rule looks in the settings file:

scene: "Evening"
at: "21:30"
days: [mon, tue, wed, thu, fri]

If a scene doesn't start the way you planned, check the “Questions” section at the bottom of the page.
